Maybe this is why you stopped

Still waiting for your MRI?

Or your gastroscopy. Or the blood tests you wanted to do properly. Or the hormone work-up you keep meaning to arrange.

For many people in their fifties and sixties, the frustration is not that healthcare does not exist. It is that the answer is always somewhere in the future: a referral, an appointment, another appointment, another result, another letter.

And then there is private care. It can be faster — but it can also feel very expensive for something that does not necessarily feel luxurious. You can spend more than a thousand pounds on a comprehensive private health assessment and still spend the rest of the month arranging the next thing yourself. Bupa's current Be.Ahead assessment, for example, is £1,199 for a three-hour assessment with a GP, blood tests, ECG, fitness testing and targeted cancer screening.

The new Thailand

Wellness is becoming part of the reason people travel.

Thailand is deliberately moving tourism towards health, wellness, meaning and longer-term wellbeing — not simply beaches, hotels and sightseeing.

In 2026, the Tourism Authority of Thailand is promoting the global Healing is the New Luxury direction, positioning wellness as a defining part of meaningful travel. Its “Healing Journey Thailand” campaign was officially launched in January 2026, with a focus on high-value, wellness-led and purpose-driven travel.

Wellness is the new tourism. Healing is the new luxury.

That is the context in which this programme sits: not a conventional holiday with a massage added, and not a hospital stay disguised as tourism.

What we are recommending to KIN next

Make the assessment deeper — where it is clinically appropriate.

The following is a recommendation for the programme under development. It is not a promise that these tests are currently included. KIN would need to confirm medical indication, availability, price and the exact clinical pathway.

RECOMMENDATION 01

Comprehensive Executive Health Assessment

Physician consultation, medical and family history, medication review, height/weight/BMI/body fat/waist, blood pressure, heart rhythm, ECG, cardiovascular and diabetes risk, glucose/HbA1c, full blood count, cholesterol, kidney/liver/thyroid function, vitamin D, B12, folate and ferritin. Where appropriate: advanced fitness assessment, VO₂ max, cardiorespiratory fitness and grip strength.

UK reference: £1,199
Reference point: Bupa Be.Ahead, a three-hour private health assessment. This is a UK benchmark, not a KIN price.
RECOMMENDATION 02

Bone health + DEXA

Consider DEXA bone-density assessment with T-score, Z-score and FRAX where clinically indicated — particularly relevant to healthy ageing and bone health in later life.

UK reference: from £150
Example London private DEXA price. Diagnostic pricing in Thailand must be confirmed by KIN. DEXA is a diagnostic scan, not a treatment for osteoporosis.
RECOMMENDATION 03

Gastrointestinal screening

Where clinically indicated, consider a pathway for gastroscopy and/or colonoscopy rather than leaving a patient to organise a separate investigation at home.

UK reference: £1,200 gastroscopy · £1,500 colonoscopy
Published London private prices shown for comparison only; histology can be extra. These are not KIN prices.
RECOMMENDATION 04

Hormone & endocrine review

Where symptoms and medical history justify it, consider an appropriate hormone/endocrine blood panel with clinician interpretation rather than selling a generic “hormone test”.

UK reference: ~£150–£300 for a broader panel
Private UK prices vary substantially by panel and interpretation. A clinician should determine what is appropriate. This is not a KIN price.
RECOMMENDATION 05

Advanced imaging — only when appropriate

A physician-guided MRI pathway could be considered for selected patients. The objective is not “scan everything”; it is to investigate a clinically relevant question properly.

UK reference: MRI services from about £200+
Published private UK prices vary by scan and provider. No MRI is promised in the Thailand programme unless KIN confirms it.

Questions worth asking

Before you enquire.

Is this a medical treatment programme?

It is presented as a preventative and restorative health and wellness programme. It is not a substitute for your GP or consultant and is not a promise to diagnose or treat a specific condition. A doctor must determine whether an individual is suitable.

Can MRI, gastroscopy, DEXA or hormone testing be added?

They are recommendations under consideration for the programme, not automatic inclusions. KIN must confirm clinical indication, availability, pathway and price for each patient.

What if I have a partner with me?

Tell us on the enquiry form. The programme source specifically identifies leisure travellers and companions as a target profile. Any clinical services for a companion would need to be quoted separately unless confirmed otherwise.

What happens after I submit my details?

Your contact details are captured first. You are then taken to a thank-you page where you can choose a free video consultation time through our calendar. You do not have to book the call immediately; your enquiry has already been received.

Is the calendar shown before I give my details?

No. The booking calendar is deliberately placed after the lead form. The first step is to tell us who you are and what you are looking for. The second step is optional: choose a video consultation time.
