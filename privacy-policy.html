2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, country, enquiry details, communication preferences, consent records and basic technical information such as IP address, device, browser, cookies and website activity.

Please do not submit medical records, diagnoses or detailed health information through a general enquiry form or ordinary messaging channel. If such information is needed, we will explain the appropriate channel and safeguards.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide information you request.
  • To arrange consultations and coordinate communication with relevant healthcare or wellness providers.
  • To explain available services, programmes, estimated costs and practical arrangements.
  • To manage our relationship with you, maintain records and improve our services.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, security and accounting requirements.
  • To send promotional or follow-up communications where we have a valid legal basis and, where required, your prior consent.

4. WhatsApp, Telephone and Automated Communications

To respond to an enquiry, we may use the contact details you provide. Depending on your choices and permissions, communication channels may include WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business, telephone calls, SMS, automated dialling or call-routing systems, automated voice assistants and voice bots, chatbots and AI-supported messaging assistants, email and video-consultation platforms.

Where you separately opt in to ongoing promotional or follow-up contact, we may contact you through the selected channels no more than once per month for marketing or general follow-up communications. This monthly limit does not apply to necessary replies, appointment arrangements or messages directly related to a specific enquiry or service you requested.

Providing a telephone number or acknowledging this Privacy Policy does not, by itself, constitute consent to marketing messages or automated marketing calls where separate consent is required by law. You may withdraw marketing consent at any time, for example by replying “STOP” or contacting us using the details in this policy.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, we process information because it is necessary to respond to your request or take steps at your request before an arrangement; because we have a legitimate interest in operating, securing and improving our services; to comply with legal obligations; or because you have given consent for a specific purpose, such as optional marketing communications or non-essential cookies.

Health information may be subject to additional legal protections. We will only process it where an applicable legal condition permits this and appropriate safeguards are in place.

We do not sell your personal information.
